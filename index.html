About

I came to UX/UI design from instructional design, where I spent years designing course experiences and navigation for learners of every age and background.

That taught me to start with the person on the other side of the screen. I'm also an artist, so I care that the work is clear, and that it's a pleasure to spend time in.

Whether you're counting to ten or managing a retirement fund, I create for whoever's on the other side of the screen.
